Our story

A school is made by its people.

Jomeg Schools is rooted in Umuagu Ibeku, Umuahia, Abia State. Its story lives not only in records, but in the children who come to learn, the families who support them and the teachers who help them find their way.

Every class, new question and shared achievement adds another chapter to that story.
