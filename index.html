Every child deserves room to become.

Jomeg Schools is in Umuagu Ibeku, Umuahia, Abia State. We believe a good education should do more than fill notebooks: it should help children grow in confidence, character and curiosity.

We want every learner to feel known, encouraged to explore, and ready to take their next step with purpose.
